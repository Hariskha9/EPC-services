Know Your Property

What Is an Energy Performance Certificate?

An Energy Performance Certificate, commonly known as an EPC, provides information about the energy efficiency of a property. It gives the property an energy efficiency rating and provides recommendations that can help improve the property's energy performance.

Do you need one? If you are selling or letting a residential property, an EPC may be required. Speak to us if you're unsure whether your property needs one.
